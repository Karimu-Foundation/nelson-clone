## Published precedents worth encoding as standing checks

1. **Maintenance is taught, and transfer waits for it.** Arri's committees struggled at first; Karimu balanced incentives with accountability, and only resumed construction in 2025 once maintenance held. Slower transfer is the accepted cost. *(2024 COO letter)*
2. **Tuberculosis work follows the disease, not the ward boundary.** The TB project was extended to neighbouring wards chosen by the Babati District Secretary of Health, because eliminating TB in three wards fails if it is widespread around them. Closed in Arri and Ayalagaya as cases fell; opened in Dabil. *(2025)*
3. **Requests outside the model go to the Social Fund, not into the model.** Hidet's half-built dispensary was completed only after the government committed to staff and run it. *(2025)*
4. **Community contribution is concrete and recorded.** For the Kifaru Juu Primary bathrooms, the community dug the foundation and supplied aggregate, sand and 30,000 bricks. *(2025)*
5. **Programs are handed over, not just built.** Hygiene education is now taught annually in every school with no Karimu involvement; maternal training is delivered across all wards without Karimu's direct involvement. Handover is the success condition. *(2025)*
