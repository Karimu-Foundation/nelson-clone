monetary compensation** — the Executive Team and Leadership Team, the COO
included, work unpaid. Every person below is named in the 2025 Annual Report.

## 5. Global volunteer team
64 year-round volunteers from 15 countries donated 9,098 hours in 2025 (57 from 15 countries and 10,035 hours in 2024; 57 from 14 countries in 2023). A further 43 volunteers from 5 countries joined trips in 2025 and completed 41 projects.

## What this means for the agent
- Volunteers are **unpaid**. Any task or timeline drafted for them must be realistic about the hours they actually have, explicit about the ask, and generous with recognition.
- "Just hire someone" is not available. Proposing paid capacity outside the Tanzanian staff departs from how Karimu operates and must be flagged as such.
- Anything touching a named individual's performance, compensation or personal situation goes to a human, never into a draft.
