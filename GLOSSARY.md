# Glossary

- **COO** — Chief Operating Officer. Nelson Mattos; overall responsibility for daily operations, chairs the weekly Leadership Team meeting.
- **Five Pillars** — Karimu's integrated development model: Sanitation & Clean Water, Health, Education, Income Generation, Financial Services.
- **Karimu Methodology / Theory of Change** — Seed → Seedling → Tree maturity model for wards, ending in a gradual exit once a ward is self-sustaining.
- **Ward** — the local administrative unit in Tanzania where Karimu runs projects (e.g., Ayasanda, Dareda Mission, Ayalagaya, Arri, Dabil).
- **MOU** — Memorandum of Understanding between Karimu, project beneficiaries, and local government, required before any project executes.
- **Executive Team** — President & Co-Founder, Treasurer & Co-Founder, COO, Advisor & Architect. Responsible for strategy.
- **Leadership Team** — Executive Team + Regional Director, Global Program Area Leads, Chiefs of Staff, CMO, and others. Meets weekly, chaired by the COO, addresses operations.
- **Tanzanian Board of Directors** — ensures legal/regulatory compliance and advises the COO on culturally-sensitive matters; meets quarterly.
- **Global Program Area Lead** — regional volunteer leadership role overseeing a program area (historically spanning USA, Israel, Brazil, and other countries).
- **Efficiency ratio** — % of donated funds that reach Tanzania/projects directly (96.3%-98.6% historically). Karimu's headline accountability metric.
- **Co-investment** — the community/government's required minimum 5% contribution (labor, materials, or money) to any project.
