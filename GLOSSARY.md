# Glossary

> **Source rule — this file is public.** Everything here is drawn from Karimu's
> published Annual Reports (2023, 2024, 2025), available at
> <https://karimufoundation.org/annual-reports>. If a fact is not in one of those
> PDFs, it does not belong in this file — it belongs in the private knowledge base.
> See `README.md` § Keeping your real knowledge private.

- **COO** — Chief Operating Officer. Nelson Mattos. Overall responsibility for daily operations; chairs the weekly Leadership Team. Unpaid.
- **Five Pillars** — Karimu's integrated model: Sanitation & Clean Water, Health, Education, Income Generation, Financial Services. Addressed together, because addressing one alleviates poverty but does not eliminate it.
- **Karimu Methodology** — the three-phase approach: Seed (selection & assessment) → Seedling (project execution) → Tree (gradual exit).
- **Theory of Change** — formalised in 2025. Maps root causes of extreme poverty to lasting community well-being, through pillar-specific projects plus governance and maintenance systems.
- **Ward** — the Tanzanian administrative unit below a district, above a village. Karimu's unit of work: Ayalagaya, Arri, Dabil, in Babati District, Manyara Region.
- **MOU** — Memorandum of Understanding between Karimu, the project's beneficiaries, and local government, allocating responsibilities. Required before any project executes.
- **Co-investment** — the minimum 5% of project cost contributed by local residents and/or government in labour, materials or money. All adults take part.
- **Executive Team** — President, Treasurer, COO, Advisor & Architect. Unpaid. Responsible for strategy.
- **Leadership Team** — Executive Team plus Regional Director, Global Program Area Leads, Chiefs of Staff, CMO and consultants. Meets weekly, chaired by the COO.
- **Tanzanian Board of Directors** — ensures compliance with Tanzanian law, helps staff with critical issues, advises the COO on culturally sensitive matters.
- **Global Program Area Lead** — volunteer role overseeing a program area across regions.
- **Karimu Social Fund** — up to 10% of funds raised, reserved for urgent requests outside the three focus wards.
- **Efficiency ratio** — share of donated funds reaching Tanzania and projects. 98.6% to Tanzania / 96.3% direct to projects in 2024; 99% transferred to Tanzania in 2025. Karimu's headline accountability metric — always cite the year.
- **"No handouts"** — Karimu's own term for refusing to build what a community has not committed to co-invest in and maintain.
